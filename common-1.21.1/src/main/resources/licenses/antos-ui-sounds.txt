All clips are adapted to mono OGG Vorbis at 44.1 kHz, shortened, faded, filtered,
and adjusted in playback speed to soften the original 1-bit harmonics.
Open and close are quieter, lower-register beeps with a 1 kHz low-pass filter.

ui/close.ogg: 42_select_3.wav, reversed, playback speed 0.65

ui/maximize.ogg: 43_select_4.wav

ui/sale.ogg: 55_old_computer.wav, 42_select_3.wav and 43_select_4.wav, lower confirmation
